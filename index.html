BiaBox

Biabox is an initiative from Enactus TU Dublin that tackles food waste by redistributing surplus food directly to households in the community. We collect ingredients that would otherwise be discarded and ensure they are put to meaningful use, reducing unnecessary emissions and supporting a more circular food system.
